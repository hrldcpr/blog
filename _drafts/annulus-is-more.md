<small>*Annulus with inner radius $$r$$ and width $$d$$.*</small>
{: style="text-align:center;"}

$$
A=2\pi rd
$$

Annulus of inner radius $$r$$ and width $$d$$:

$$
A=\pi(r+d)^2-\pi r^2=2\pi rd+\pi d^2
$$

So the annulus is a ring plus a circle.
