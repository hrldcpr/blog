$$37|73$$

Earlier this year, my brother turned 37 while my mom was 73. My mom, being a lover of numbers, immediately pointed out this fun symmetry.

So I wondered when this "reverse twin" phenomenon would happen with _me_ and my mom. But it turns out it never will 😭

---

$$
XY|YX
$$

The base-10 number "XY" represents the value $$10X+Y$$ and the base-10 number "YX" represents $$10Y+X$$.

So the difference between "XY" and "YX" is:

$$
(10X+Y)-(10Y+X)=9X-9Y=9(X-Y)
$$

That is, the difference between reversed decimal numbers is always a multiple of 9.

**Two people can be reverse twins if and only if their ages differ by a multiple of 9.**

My age and my mom's age always differ by either 35 years (between our birthdays) or 34 years, neither of which is a multiple of 9, so we will never be reverse twins 😭

---

$$
😭|😭
$$

Was this the only time my mom and brother will be reverse twins? In 11 years, both digits will have changed by the same amount, and thus will match again---i.e. 37 and 73 goes to 48 and 84. And in fact it happened 11 years ago too, I guess we just didn't notice!

For them, every pair of reversed ages so far has been:

$$
04 | 40 \\
15 | 51 \\
26 | 62 \\
37 | 73
$$

Here's to many more!

But good news, it turns out my _dad_ and I are reverse twins:

$$
03 | 30 \\
14 | 41 \\
25 | 52 \\
36 | 63
$$

We just didn't notice because he's not as into numbers.

---

$$
👯|👯
$$

I look forward to finding more of my reverse twins, and celebrating our rare alignments.

If you've read this far, try finding some of your reverse twins!

And note that if you're the same age as someone: zero is a multiple of 9, so you'll be reverse twins every 11 years:

$$
00|00 \\
11|11 \\
22|22 \\
33|33 \\
\dots
$$
